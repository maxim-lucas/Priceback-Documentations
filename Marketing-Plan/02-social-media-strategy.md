# Social Media Strategy

## Platform priority

1. **TikTok** — primary growth engine. Deal/finance content has strong algorithmic reach for new accounts (unlike IG, TikTok doesn't require an existing follower base to go viral). Canadian TikTok has an active "money saving" niche.
2. **Instagram** (`@priceback.ca`, already claimed) — Reels mirror TikTok content 1:1 (repurpose, don't recreate); Stories/Highlights become the "proof wall" (screenshots of real refunds, receipts, notifications); Feed becomes evergreen educational carousels.
3. **Facebook** — not a content platform for us, a **distribution** platform: join/post in existing Costco Canada / deal-hunting / coupon Facebook groups (huge, active, older-skewing audience exactly matched to Costco shoppers). Low content investment, high placement value.
4. Watch-list (not v1): Reddit (r/Costco, r/PersonalFinanceCanada — organic only, no paid/spam posting, community-run), YouTube Shorts (auto-cross-post from TikTok once volume exists).

## Content pillars (rotate across all pillars weekly)

1. **"Did you know" hooks** — e.g. "Costco owes you money right now and you don't know it" / "This item dropped $8 this week — did you buy it?" Pure curiosity-gap hook, ends on app CTA.
2. **Proof-of-refund** — screen recordings of real (consented) refund notifications firing, dollar amounts overlaid. This is the highest-converting format — it's the product demoing itself.
3. **Educational / policy explainer** — "How Costco's price adjustment policy actually works" (most people don't know it exists). Positions PriceBack as the tool that makes an existing right usable.
4. **Weekly flyer drop content** — "This week's biggest Costco price drops in Canada" as a native carousel/Reel, sourced directly from the flyer-scan pipeline ([[flyer-scan-multipage-national]]) — this is content the product already generates as data, just needs a template wrapper.
5. **UGC re-shares** — repost real users' refund screenshots (with permission) as Stories/Reels. Costs nothing to produce, highest trust signal.
6. **Meme/relatable frugal-culture content** — low-effort, high-frequency filler between the above (grocery inflation memes, "me checking if my receipt owes me money" formats).

## Format rules

- Every video ≤ 30s, hook in first 1.5s (text-on-screen, no slow intros).
- Every post ends with a single clear CTA: link in bio → app store / website.
- Captions always include a Canadian-specific angle (province, CAD amounts) — differentiates from generic US deal content.
- Post cadence target: TikTok/Reels daily (7/week), IG feed 3x/week, IG Stories daily, Facebook group posts 2-3x/week (native, non-promotional tone, value-first).

## Tone / voice

Friendly, slightly cheeky, "your money-savvy friend" — not corporate fintech, not coupon-clipping-grandma. Think: the energy of a good roommate group chat, not a bank's Instagram.
